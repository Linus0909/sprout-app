Sprout — Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Sprout is a personal budgeting app. This page explains what information it collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: the username and password you choose. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they're hashed (PBKDF2-SHA256, salted) before being saved.
  • Budget data you enter: your monthly income, spending limits, expenses you log, and savings goals you create.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't run ads or third-party analytics/tracking inside the app.
  • We don't access your real bank accounts or move real money — Sprout is a manual tracker only.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored in a database operated by , the developer of Sprout. It is used solely to provide the app's functionality to you.

Your controls

From the Settings screen inside the app you can, at any time:

  • View a full export of your personal data
  • Download your data as a JSON file
  • Change your password
  • Permanently delete your account, which immediately and irreversibly deletes all associated data
